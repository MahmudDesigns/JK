# John Kamau - Thika Town MP 2027 Campaign Website

The official, mobile-first, minimalist campaign website for **John Kamau**, candidate for Member of Parliament (MP) for Thika Town Constituency in the 2027 Kenyan General Election. Operating under the banner **"The People's Voice — Voice of the Mwananchi."**

This platform is engineered to outperform traditional, static political websites by leveraging high-performance minimalist design, lightning-fast mobile optimization, and interactive grassroots voter-engagement features.

## 🚀 Key Features

* **Grassroots Narrative Timeline:** Chronological storytelling highlighting John's genuine journey from Athena and Kiandutu, driving a Tuk-Tuk for 5 years, leading student government at Thika Technical, to serving as an educator.
* **The Three Pillars Manifesto Grid:** Simple, highly readable, actionable presentation of core campaign commitments: Poverty Eradication, Community Empowerment via TVET Skills, and Protective Legislation for local traders.
* **Interactive "Voice Your Issue" Widget:** Built-in voter polling panel allowing local residents to instantly input community concerns, creating a direct data feedback loop.
* **Frictionless M-Pesa Integration:** Secure, clean, and highly visible payment card framework for transparent grassroots campaign fundraising.
* **Floating WhatsApp Gateway:** Seamless, persistent floating interaction point targeting tech-savvy youth voters for direct communication.

## 🎨 Design Language

* **Primary Background:** Clean, high-contrast `#FFFFFF` White for structural readability and speed.
* **Primary Typography/Containers:** Premium `#002147` Oxford Blue representing authority, stability, and clean leadership.
* **Accent Highlights:** Energetic Teal (`#008080` / `#00A8A8`) to draw instant focus to conversion items, active buttons, and milestones.
